Modern warfare no longer takes place only on the battlefield, but within consciousness, language, and memory. It seeps into everyday life, reshaping how individuals understand themselves under conditions of ongoing threat. Within this reality, identity loses its stability and becomes a continuous process of revision, erasure, and reinvention.
